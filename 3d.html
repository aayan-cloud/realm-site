Questions05 / 05
Do you need the product in front of you?

No. We model from your product photos or the maker's spec. The white PC built for Tech Homan was modelled from the maker's photos.

What will it not do?

It is not a shop and not a configurator. Nothing goes in a cart, nothing is priced live, and the visitor cannot swap parts. The prices in the inspector are shown with the date they were checked. If you need any of that, say so when you message us and we will tell you whether we can do it and what it costs.

How long does it take?

We do not put a number here because it depends on how many parts the product has. The delivery date goes in the quote, in writing, before any work starts.

Why $4,000 when a website is $1,000?

The website is one page, written from scratch and live in a week. This is every part of your product modelled in 3D, rendered frame by frame in Blender, and a page built around those frames.

What happens after delivery?

You pay, and the model files and the page are yours. There is no monthly fee and no retainer. If a part or a price changes later, send us the new one and we quote the update. If you would rather change it yourself, you have the files.

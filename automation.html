Questions05 / 05
Does it send the messages for me?

No. Every message is drafted, never sent automatically. You read it, change it if you want, and send it yourself. It is your name on it.

Where do the leads come from?

Google Maps, for businesses with plenty of reviews and no website, pointed at your city and trade. Each one is checked before it reaches you.

Is it fully automatic?

No. Some steps run on their own and some we run by hand, and every lead is checked. The figures on this page are recorded results from real runs, not a live feed.

What do I own after delivery?

An automation or an agent is yours. The files are handed over when you pay on delivery. The lead engine is different. It is a monthly service, $299 a month after the $500 setup. You get the checked list each month, not the engine itself.

Why does automation say from $2,500?

Because we do not know what yours needs to do yet. Tell us the business and the job, and you get a fixed price and a delivery date in writing before any work starts. If it needs to answer the phone, that is the AI receptionist, priced separately at $499 setup, then $149 a month.
